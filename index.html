Our Story

Every memory
blooms beautifully

Cherry Blossom was born from a simple belief — that a gift is never just an object. It is a frozen moment of love, an emotion carefully wrapped, a story told without words.

We are a luxury gifting boutique that curates experiences, not just products. Each arrangement, each hamper, each personalised piece is thoughtfully designed to make the recipient feel extraordinarily seen — because they are.

Crafted with intention
Every piece is hand-selected by our curators who believe beauty lives in the details.
Delivered with love
From packaging to presentation, every step honours the emotion behind the gift.
Memories over moments
We create things that stay long after the occasion fades into a beautiful memory.
